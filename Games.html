Games I enjoy


Games that I played through my childhood






Even with the picture showing only Destiny 2, this also applies to the first game. The Destiny series was one of the first games I ever played and still play to this day. Having this game with me for over 10 years of my life made me love the ins and outs of this game.


In the 10 years I have played this game, I have learn a lot about the universe and the characters that inhabit it. Being a part of this universe as the guardian (the player) I ended up being way to attached to these characters than I realized. To the point that at the ending of the "Final Shape" storyline, I couldn't help but get a bit emotional after seeing the guardian and the other characters growth at the end of this 10 year saga. Just for it to start anew in this new saga coming up.



Terraria is a game I played since my childhood, my first sandbox game, even before minecraft. This is the first game I played that I could truly express my creativity. The problem was I'm not very creative. Apart from the sandbox features, the progression in a world is enjoyable as you can progress in many ways. In fact there are only a few mandatory bosses out of the whole game.


I still play this game to this day, just not that constantly. I do go through a playthrough maybe every other month. It is a fun game that changes every world you make as even a few select bosses even change depending on the world.






Persona 5 Royal is an amazing game that can last you for days just for the most basic playthrough. This game builds up its world and its individual characters so much. This is also a game that loves its symbolism, if you are a fan of tarot cards or certain gods representing what certain characters believe in or their backstory.


The game has two diffrent parts to it, the life sim part. Where you make friends, get a job, work on yourself as a person. The other half is is about fighting other peoples physical manifestations of emotions. Which sounds weird but has some nice turn based combat that can be difficult. The part I like the most is how the two gameplay loops that are complete oppisite of each other helps each other. Being good at the life sim makes you stronger during the combat portion, while the combat is the best way to earn money which makes the life sim so much easier.
