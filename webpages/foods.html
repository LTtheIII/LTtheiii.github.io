Tonkastu


The recipe for a good Tonkastu
You can go find the recipe in more detail by clicking the image.




Even If you can just summarize this as a fried pork, its still a delicious thats easy to make and doesn't need to many ingredients. The nice part is you can do the breading process of this recipe to anything you want, so this recipe can get you some pretty crispy fried chicken as well!

The website I got this recipe from also has a good sauce to go with, and many more recipes. I recommend you go look around it yourself!



The Ingredients

  • Porkchops
  • Eggs
  • Flour
  • Panko bread crumbs
  • oil

The steps

  1. Pound the pork chop to 3/4 inches thick, then cover in flour
  2. mix an egg and a tablespoon of flour, this makes a wonderful wet batter
  3. take the flour covered pork chop and dip into the egg batter, then press into panko (just hard enough so the panko sticks)
  4. heat some oil to 340 degrees F over medium heat, and cook each side for about 5 minutes
