Music That I like

My music taste might not really be with many people, but I like them.




"Beneath the Mask"



A song from Persona 5, also in persona 5 royal. The lyrics are nice and calming, having multiple versions with rain in the backround. Many people use this song to help relaxe, and to study. One of the many good songs to why people say persona have good songs. It might have symbolism in this song, I haven't really tried to learn the symbolism of the songs. This is sung by Lyn, the main singer for almost every song in persona 5. I recommend her other songs.



"The Brave"



The opening song for the anime "Frieren Beyond Journey's End", though it only shows half the song if you listen from the show youtube has the full song. The music video itself is nice, and along with the lyrics it perfectly shows the type of story the show is. Made by Yoasobi, they make a lot of good songs in both japanese and english. If you like this you should go check out their other works



"Rainy Boots"



This is an english cover by the orginal by the same name by inabakumori, and is covered by Will Stetson. This song is a nice case of me not really listening to it for the message, or lyrics. I just really like how the song flows and is another relaxation song. A lot of inabakumori's songs also play like this, so if you like this song you should check it out. Will Stetson also does covers on a wide range of songs, which could be worth looking into.
