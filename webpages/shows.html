Shows That I enjoyed watching




While the classic Devil May Cry fans seemed to not enjoy this show, I still found it nice. It may not have been that faithful to the source material, but still had an story worth following. The animation was really good as well, I don't see shows that manage to mix CGI and hand-drawn scenes so well.

Truth be told im just a sucker for action shows, and this show delivers on action with some well timed jokes. The character design also works well with each other as dante will often comment on them.



Frieren Beyond Journey's End is a amazing story, that takes placed after the story. The story takes place in your generic fantasy world; swords, magic, a hero, and a demon lord. This story starts off with the demon lord already dead, and the purpose of the hero and his party is done. So instead it focuses on the elf, Frieren, and her joureny discovering the importance of connections with others. As she is one of the last elfs, her lifespan is much greater than everyone else, so she becomes detached.

Frieren only seeing how important others can make life nicer only after the death of the hero.



Overlord is a generic story where someone is transported into their game, and they are so overpowered to where you never have to question if they will lose a fight. The fun part is how the main character, Ainz, goes about his plans. He could just brute force all his foes down, but instead he stays careful and works in the shadows.

Ainz may be strong and smart, but his intelligence only helps with his own plans. When he was transported the NPCs that was also in his castle became sentient. A few of them smarter than Ainz, and with them working independently now, Ainz have to guess what they are planning. Usually messing up the plan but still impressing everyone. Its not a story to where you ask if they will make it through something, but more how.
